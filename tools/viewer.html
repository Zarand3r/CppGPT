Logit lens — where the prediction forms

The residual stream at each layer, read through the final layernorm and the tied unembedding: what would the model predict if it stopped here?

The lens borrows the final layernorm for every layer, but each layer's residual has its own scale, so early layers are distorted. Belrose et al.'s tuned lens fixes this with a learned per-layer probe; this is the plain lens, so read early layers as indicative, not quantitative.

Residual stream norm

‖residual‖ per layer at the selected position. Flat segments are layers that barely move the representation.

Attention

Each cell is one head: rows are query positions, columns are keys. Lower-triangular by construction — a token can only attend to itself and its past.

Attention is routing, not explanation. A bright cell means information moved, not that it mattered. Jain & Wallace (2019) and Wiegreffe & Pinter (2019) both show attention weights can be altered substantially without changing the prediction. Reading a story off these maps is the classic mistake; use them to form hypotheses that a causal method — ablation, patching — then has to confirm.
